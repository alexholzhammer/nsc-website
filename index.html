Got questions?

The stuff people always ask before they sign up.

What's the pricing?

It depends on your room, and every price is all-inclusive — food, drinks, activities and transport are all in. Rooms run from $499 (Dorm or Tented Camp) up to $949 for a private Premium room. Full breakdown in Rooms & Pricing above.

What's included?

Your room, every meal, all your drinks (beer, wine and soft drinks), snacks, every activity and game, and transport to the venue. You show up and join in — that's it.

What's not included?

Getting yourself to Cape Town (flights are on you), travel insurance — which you'll need to attend — visas, and personal spending money. Oh, and post-Camp therapy for the Camp Life Withdrawal.

What's the refund policy?

The deposit is $99, and the balance is due by 12 December 2026. Everything you've paid is fully refundable if you cancel on or before 12 December 2026 — after that date, payments aren't refundable.

Can I work during Camp?

Short answer: no. If you have to fire off an email or a Slack reply, nobody is going to judge you — but the whole point is a long weekend to unplug, connect, have fun and rack up Camp points. Camp vibes are non-work vibes.

Who usually comes to NSC?

Full-time nomads, part-time nomads, founders, developers, writers, people at remote-friendly companies, and anyone who just loves to travel. Past Campers have come from a dozen countries across Europe, North America and beyond.

Am I nomad enough?

The “Nomad” in Nomad Summer Camp is about mindset, not mileage — people who love new experiences and roll with the punches when things don't go perfectly. Full-time on the road, a few months a year, or somewhere in between: you're nomad enough. For the record, all four founders are full-time nomads.

What are the different ticket options?

Every ticket is all-inclusive — three nights of accommodation, all meals and drinks, and access to every game, activity and party. Rooms come in three tiers — Premium, Standard and Budget — and the Premium and Standard rooms let you pick a per-person shared rate or pay more for a private room. Wherever you sleep, you get the same all-access pass.

How do I reach a human?

Email counselor@nomadsummercamp.com, or DM us on Instagram @nomad.summercamp — we usually reply there fastest.
