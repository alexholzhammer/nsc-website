What we collect and why

  • Camp sign-ups (Tally) — when you use a “Book Your Spot” or camper form, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, and any answers you provide. We use these to process your booking and contact you about your place at Camp. Legal basis: taking steps to enter into a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in managing sign-ups.
  • Deposits and payments (Stripe) — when you pay a deposit or other amount, Stripe processes your card and payment details. We never see or store full card numbers; we receive confirmation of payment and limited transaction data. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Newsletter (Beehiiv) — if you sign up for our newsletter, we store your email address and basic engagement data, such as whether an email was opened or a link clicked, so we can send Camp announcements. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Messages you send us — if you email counselor@nomadsummercamp.com, we keep your message and contact details to reply and for our records. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4) — if you accept analytics cookies, we collect pseudonymous usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location from a shortened IP address, device and browser type, and how you reached the site. We use this to understand what’s useful and improve the site. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Server logs (hosting) — our host records standard technical data such as your IP address, browser, and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in security and reliable delivery.

Cookies and consent

We show a consent banner on your first visit. Essential cookies needed to run the site are always on. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) and cookies set by embedded YouTube videos load only if you accept them. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the cookie settings link on the site.

Embedded content and fonts

  • YouTube — some pages embed YouTube videos and load video thumbnails from Google. Where possible we use YouTube’s no-cookie mode, but playing a video or loading certain embeds causes Google to set cookies and receive your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — our pages load fonts from Google’s servers, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.

International data transfers

We are based in the United States, and several of our providers (Stripe, Beehiiv, Google, GitHub) process data there. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-up and booking data: for the duration of the relevant Camp cycle and for up to 7 years afterwards, to cover our accounting and legal obligations and any possible claims.
  • Payment records: 7 years, as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Messages you send us: up to 3 years after the matter is closed.
  • Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 90 days.
  • Analytics data: according to our Google Analytics retention setting, up to 14 months.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days, for security purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • receive a copy in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you may request access to or deletion of your personal information and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights; we do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any of these rights, email counselor@nomadsummercamp.com.

Children

Camp is an adults-only experience. This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security
